Frequently asked questions

How do I add a place?

  • Tap the Add Place tile (+) on a page and enter it yourself.
  • In a maps app such as Google Maps, Apple Maps, Naver Map, or KakaoMap, or in Instagram, tap Share → HowTogo to save it right away. If HowTogo isn’t in the list, tap More at the end of the app row in the share sheet and turn it on.

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold an empty area of the Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget in the top-left corner, and choose HowTogo. You can add it to the Lock Screen too.

Can I see my places on my other devices?

Devices signed in to iCloud with the same Apple Account stay in sync automatically. If your places don’t appear, check that:

  • you’re signed in under Settings → [your name] → iCloud,
  • you’re connected to the internet,
  • and you’ve given it a few minutes — the first sync can take a while.

Will I lose my places if I delete and reinstall the app?

If you were using iCloud, your places come back when you reinstall with the same Apple Account. Make sure Settings → Backup → iCloud Sync is on. If you don’t use iCloud, the app’s data is included in your iPhone backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup).

How do I send a page to a friend?

Tap the button at the bottom left to open Page Settings, then tap Share This Page to send it as a file through Messages, AirDrop, or another app. When your friend opens the file in HowTogo, the page is added. Visited marks aren’t included.

Directions open in the wrong maps app.

Choose the app you want under gear button → Settings → Maps App. If the app isn’t installed on your device, directions open on the web.

How many pages can I create?

You can create up to 2 pages for free. Buy Unlimited Pages once to create as many as you like. It’s not a subscription, so you won’t be charged again.

My purchase doesn’t show up on a new device.

Tap gear button → Settings → Page → Restore Purchase. Make sure you’re signed in with the Apple Account you used to buy it.

How do I get a refund?

App Store purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
