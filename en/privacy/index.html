HowTogo Privacy Policy

Effective: November 27, 2026

HowTogo (the “app”) is an iOS app for collecting the places you want to visit into pages. The developer of the app (the “developer”) does not collect your personal data. This policy explains where information in the app is stored and where it is sent.

1. Information the developer collects

The developer does not collect any personal data and does not run any servers.

  • There are no accounts or sign-ins.
  • The app uses no ads, analytics, or tracking tools.
  • The app does not ask for access to your location.

2. Where the information you enter is stored

The page titles, place names, addresses, notes, links, visited marks, and background photos you enter are stored only in these two places.

LocationDetails
Your deviceStorage on your device shared by the app, its widget, and its share extension
Your own iCloudIf you’re signed in to iCloud, your data is kept in your private iCloud storage from Apple and synced between devices that use the same Apple Account. The developer cannot see this data.

For background photos, only the single photo you choose from your library is passed to the app. The app does not access the rest of your photo library.

3. Information sent to other services

Some features send only the information they need to the services below. The developer does not receive or keep this information.

FeatureSent toInformation sent
iCloud syncAppleThe data you enter (stored in your own account)
Finding locations from addresses, calculating travel times for routesApple MapsPlace addresses or coordinates

Each service handles information according to its own privacy policy.

  • Apple: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
  • Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Information you send yourself

When you create a file with “Share This Page” and send it through Messages, AirDrop, the Files app, or another app, the file contains that page’s title, place names, addresses, coordinates, notes, links, and background photo. You decide who receives the file, and the developer never receives it. (Page-share files don’t include visited marks.)

5. Retention and deletion

  • Your data stays on your device and in your iCloud until you delete it.
  • When you delete a place or page in the app, it’s deleted from both your device and iCloud.
  • Deleting the app removes the data on your device. To remove the data kept in iCloud, go to Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage on your iPhone and choose HowTogo.

6. Children’s privacy

Because the app doesn’t collect anyone’s personal data, it doesn’t collect personal data from children.

7. Your rights

Your data exists only on your device and in your iCloud, so you can view, change, or delete it in the app at any time. If you have questions about privacy, please contact us below.
